# Garden Rounded Planter Turnaround v1 Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production concept turnaround sheet for a single 3D mobile-game garden planter / cover obstacle, intended for image-to-3D reconstruction.

Input image: Image 1 is a style, palette, and proportion reference. Focus specifically on the cute chunky rounded planter obstacles in the center of the arena. Do not recreate the full arena.

Primary request: Design one new modular garden planter obstacle that matches the reference much more closely than a long narrow wall. It must look short, thick, plump, rounded, cute, and pastel.

Subject and proportions:

- One low rounded-rectangle planter, like a soft chunky capsule-shaped block.
- Footprint about 1.55:1 length-to-depth, clearly wide and deep rather than long and skinny.
- Height about 0.65 of its depth, with a flat stable base.
- Very large rounded corners and exaggerated soft bevels.
- Thick domed cushion of fresh grass on top, with only a few tiny pink and yellow flowers.
- Body made from oversized smooth rounded stone panels in pastel lavender, warm cream, and muted aqua-teal.
- Small simple turquoise medallion and restrained warm-gold trim on the front.
- Strong simple silhouette readable from a top-down game camera.
- No thin fragile pieces, no floating parts, no dense vines.

Sheet layout:

- Wide 16:9 professional prop turnaround sheet on a clean pale warm-gray background.
- The exact same object shown consistently in five views.
- One large front three-quarter hero view on the left.
- Four smaller clean orthographic views on the right: front, side, back, and top.
- All views fully visible with generous spacing and identical geometry, colors, flower placement, and ornament placement.
- Subtle soft grounding shadow only, no environment scene.
- No labels, arrows, dimensions, or text.

Style and rendering:

- Polished stylized 3D game-prop concept art with a hand-painted material feel.
- Bright cheerful pastel palette, soft diffuse studio lighting, and lifted shadows.
- Clean soft forms, toy-like charm, and smooth readable material separation.
- Preserve surface detail without dark grime or heavy ambient occlusion.
- Suitable as a clear modeling reference for Tripo image-to-3D.

Avoid: long skinny bench proportions, sharp corners, realistic gritty stone, dull olive or muddy brown colors, neon oversaturation, dark dramatic lighting, complex foliage, excessive flowers, asymmetrical geometry drift between views, perspective distortion in orthographic views, extra props, characters, scenery, logos, watermarks, or any text.
